Lumora — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026 · Last updated: September 3, 2026

Lumora is offline-first. Your puzzles, progress and settings stay on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not create user accounts.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Game progress & daily history — puzzles solved, board state, stars, hints used, solve times. Stored in lumora.db (Room). Used to restore your game, show stats and archive. Never transmitted by us.
  • Settings & streak — sound, haptics, contrast, motion, timer, reminder opt-in, streak counters. Stored in settings.pb and streak.pb (Proto DataStore). Local only.
  • Daily reminder (opt-in) — if you enable “Daily reminder”, WorkManager schedules a local notification at 20:00. Requires POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to re-schedule after reboot. No data leaves the device.

Permissions used: VIBRATE (haptic feedback), plus the two above only when you opt in. No location, contacts, camera or microphone.

2. Advertising (AdMob) — only after consent

Lumora shows banner ads only in menus and one optional rewarded ad to reveal a hint (100% opt-in). No interstitial after every puzzle.

  • SDK: Google Mobile Ads (GMA) + User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent.
  • Banners/interstitials/rewarded are loaded only after ConsentGate.ensureThen confirms canRequestAds == true. Before consent, no ad request is made.
  • In the EEA/UK, UMP shows a consent form when required; you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy options (shown only where required).
  • AdMob may collect the AD_ID and device signals to serve ads per Google’s policies. Lumora does not add its own identifiers.
  • Remove ads forever with the one-time purchase Lumora Plus (Google Play Billing). When premium is active, no ad is requested at all (banner slot is 0 dp).

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Lumora Plus is a one-time in-app purchase via Google Play Billing. Purchase verification and restoration are handled by Google Play. Lumora stores only a local isPremium flag; no payment data is stored by us.

4. Sharing — user-initiated only

The Share button creates an emoji grid of your result (no board solution) and opens the system share sheet. Nothing is shared automatically or sent to our servers; you choose the destination app each time.

5. Backups & deletion

  • Android Auto Backup / cloud backup includes only lumora.db, settings.pb and streak.pb. Volatile files (ads.preferences_pb, zen_stats, ads_store.pb, live_session.pb) are explicitly excluded.
  • Delete all data: uninstall the app, or System Settings → Apps → Lumora → Storage → Clear data. Because data never left the device, deletion is complete.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@zrano.dev — or open an issue in the app’s Play Store listing contact.

7. Changes

We will update this file and the date above when Lumora adds networking, analytics or new data types. The version in assets/privacy.html is the in-app source of truth.

This short in-app policy is for transparency inside Settings → Credits → Privacy Policy. The Play Console Data Safety declaration (owner-maintained) is the store-facing disclosure.
