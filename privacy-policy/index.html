Quiet Focus privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-08-27

This policy covers the Android app Quiet Focus: Pomodoro ("the app"), published by iggykimi. The app is a focus timer. It works without an account and without a server of its own.

Data the app keeps on your device

The app stores your session history, your settings, your purchase status and the state of a running timer in the app's own storage on your device. This data never leaves your device as a result of the app's own code. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Android Auto Backup is configured not to back up the state of a running timer or your consent choices.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google collects and processes data for that, which can include your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions (such as views and clicks) and diagnostic information. In the European Economic Area the app shows a consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change or withdraw that consent at any time from the app's privacy options or from your device settings.

You can read how Google uses data from partners at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can limit ad personalization by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers a one-time purchase that removes ads. The purchase and its receipt are handled by Google Play. The app only stores on your device the fact that the purchase exists, so it can hide ads and restore the purchase after a reinstall. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment data.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications: to show the end-of-block alert and the ongoing timer notification.
  • Vibration: for the optional haptic cue.
  • Alarms: to fire the alert at the exact end of a block while the screen is off.
  • Network: to load ads and to complete purchases.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or any other personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is published for a teen and older audience.
